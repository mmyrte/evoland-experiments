# Why CH2025, and what is missing

**Climate CH2025 DAILY-GRIDDED** has the best current, high res (1 km), daily, bias-corrected climate data for Switzerland.

- needs to be daily for eventual coupling to wasim
- A transient set of runs would be preferable, but those are not available, unless we go back to CH2018 and thus CMIP5 data.
- CH2025 didn't have EURO-CORDEX CMIP6 available yet, so they mixed CMIP6 GCMs with CMIP5 RCMs and produced 30y climatologies at three different global warming levels (GWL): 1.5, 2, and 3 deg above preindustrial.
- The GWL-based structure is adequate for deriving decadal land-use predictors: SSP trajectories can be linked by mapping each SSP decade to the corresponding global warming level
- That there isn't a GWL4 is probably because (a) politics and (b) not enough ensemble members/years reaching this level. this is problematic for SSP5-8.5 towards the end of the century, but we can also stick with GWL3 for that for now.

| Net Primary Production on Land as Carbon Mass Flux <br>(npp)                     | g C m-2 yr-1   | Production of carbon' means the production of biomass expressed as the mass of carbon which it contains. Net primary production is the excess of gross primary production (rate of synthesis of biomass from inorganic precursors) by autotrophs ('producers') for example photosynthesis in plants or phytoplankton over the rate at which the autotrophs themselves respire some of this biomass. 'Productivity' means production per unit area. The phrase 'expressed_as' is used in the construction A_expressed_as_B where B is a chemical constituent of A. It means that the quantity indicated by the standard name is calculated solely with respect to the B contained in A neglecting all other chemical constituents of A.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
